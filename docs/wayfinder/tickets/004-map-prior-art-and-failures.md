# Map prior art, products, and abandoned attempts

## Question

What academic systems, commercial products, open-source projects, and patents
have attempted finger-driven keyless text entry, and what does their survival
or abandonment reveal?

Build a dated landscape that separates shipped products from demos, papers,
crowdfunding campaigns, patents, and inactive repositories. Record interaction
method, hardware, layout, claimed and independently measured performance,
availability, price, last meaningful activity, failure or abandonment evidence,
and source quality. Trace especially close descendants or alternatives to
LapCal. Do not infer a failure reason when the source does not state one.

The result must identify reusable ideas, repeated failure modes, and claims that
need replication rather than repetition.

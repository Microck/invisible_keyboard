# Cost viable prototype paths

## Question

What would it cost now to build evidence-producing prototypes for the credible
sensing architectures?

Create distributor-backed, dated BOM scenarios for at least a cheapest decisive
experiment and a robust development prototype. Include sensors, controller,
radio or cable, power, charging and protection, PCB or breakout boards,
mechanical attachment, fabrication, spare parts, shipping assumptions,
consumables, and required tools not normally included in the build. State
currency, region, stock status, quantity breaks, and excluded equipment.

Include a sensitivity case for the historic EUR 200 ceiling, but do not make it
the governing requirement until ticket 001 resolves it. Separate evidence cost
from a speculative production unit cost.

# Source provenance

## Gist

The canonical implementation follows a documented independent-implementation
and no-artifact-reuse policy. The local `research/` corpus supplies facts and
comparison evidence only. It supplies no code, data, model, CAD, PCB, or
calibration artifact to the product path. This ledger does not claim a strict
clean-room process or a legal safe harbor.

## Excluded material

The canonical implementation excludes:

- LapCal source code, PCB files, CAD, datasets, models, and calibration values;
- any dataset or model without a separate source and rights record;
- scraped typing text without a redistribution grant; and
- private typed content, passwords, or inferred text telemetry.

The deterministic calibration prompt corpus is project-authored. A release
must retain its content hash in the acquisition and calibration artifacts.

This provenance rule is narrower than the uses that CC BY-NC-SA may permit.
Whether a particular reproduction or adaptation needs permission depends on
the rights involved, the license terms, and applicable exceptions or
limitations. The policy is an implementation boundary, not legal advice.

## New evidence rule

Every captured session must bind its exact canonical input identity. Axis
commissioning binds the versioned pre-characterization candidate projection
and produces non-authorizing selected raw evidence. Its fixed pose order and
reducer contract live in the assembly baseline. Operational sessions require
the matching evidence-bound acquisition manifest; a candidate-bound axis
session cannot replace one.
Surface fitting and held-out validation also require the canonical surface plan,
clock fits, raw session, and plan-bound schema-v9 capture journal. The journal
stores observed facts. The plan owns each prompt's position or null meaning, so
a caller cannot relabel facts by supplying a second target table. Admission
reconstructs every display time from the bound plan start, canonical offset,
and prior acknowledged render delay. It also reconstructs every selected
16-record truth window from the raw session and rejects intra-session overlap.

Every schema-v4 fitted artifact must name its layout, feature schema,
acquisition manifest, source sessions, fitting journal hash, and derived fitting
evidence hash. Evidence sidecars, standalone matching-rule files, display
receipt logs, and validation reports are diagnostic outputs. They are not
canonical fitting or validation inputs. Runtime rederives the held-out result
from its artifact-bound plan, journal, clocks, and raw session, then compares
the cached report before it opens a controller or output device.

These hashes prove internal byte identity and consistency only. They do not
prove that a wearable was mounted as declared, that a browser cue was visible,
or that recorded samples came from the claimed physical action. A physical
study still needs an honest capture operator and independent attestation. A
missing or mismatched identity fails closed, but a matching identity is not
physical-authenticity proof.
